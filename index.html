Deskew compensates each point for the vehicle's motion during the 99.5 ms sweep. The correction grows with range (a rotation of one degree moves a point at 30 m by half a metre) and with speed, so the difference is large during fast straight driving and during turns, and negligible at walking pace. The camera lag is adjustable because the measured value for the 21 July clip rests on an inferred clip start — slide it and watch when moving objects line up. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
